## How We Protect Your Information

We use secure networks and limit access to personal information to authorized individuals only. When you access your account or make a purchase, your data is transmitted via secure servers.

While we use reasonable security measures, no method of online transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure, and absolute protection cannot be guaranteed.

If you create an account, you are responsible for maintaining your password confidentiality. Notify us immediately of any unauthorized access.

In the event of a data breach, we will inform you by email, phone, or in writing, as permitted by law.

Analytics data is anonymized where possible and never used to personally identify you. However, data linked to an IP address or customer account may be considered personal and is treated as such.
